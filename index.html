About the Host

Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology (SRIT)

Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology (SRIT), an Autonomous Institution affiliated with Anna University, is a premier engineering hub in Coimbatore. Managed by the SNR Sons Charitable Trust and accredited with an 'A' grade by NAAC, SRIT offers world-class infrastructure and an industry-aligned curriculum. The institute is dedicated to transforming students into technically competent and ethically strong professionals ready to tackle global engineering challenges.

ASME Student Section at SRIT

The ASME Student Section at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology (SRIT), Coimbatore, is a premier organization dedicated to empowering mechanical engineering students through innovation, leadership, and professional development. Recognized globally for excellence, the section achieved a distinguished rank during 2021-2022 and has consistently earned prestigious accolades including:

  • ASME Student Section Recognition Program's Distinguished Student Section Award
  • Faculty All Star Award
  • Outstanding Student Section Advisor Award
