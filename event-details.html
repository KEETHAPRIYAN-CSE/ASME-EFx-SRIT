Oral Competition

Sponsored by ASME Old Guard Committee

Overview

The Oral Competition is a prestigious event where participants showcase their technical knowledge, presentation skills, and innovative engineering solutions. This competition provides a platform for students to present their research, projects, or innovative concepts to a panel of expert judges.

Competition Format

  • Individual presentations (10-15 minutes)
  • Q&A session with expert panel (5-7 minutes)
  • Topic: Any engineering innovation or research project
  • Multimedia presentations allowed (PowerPoint, videos, demos)

Judging Criteria

  • Technical content and accuracy (40%)
  • Presentation skills and clarity (30%)
  • Innovation and creativity (20%)
  • Response to questions (10%)

Prizes & Recognition

  • Cash prizes for top 3 winners
  • Certificates from ASME
  • Recognition at the closing ceremony
  • Opportunity to network with industry professionals

Eligibility

Open to all undergraduate and graduate engineering students. Prior registration required.

Vibe Coding

Overview

Vibe Coding is a dynamic and energetic coding challenge that combines technical problem-solving with creativity and innovation. This event is designed to test your coding skills in a fun, collaborative environment where rhythm meets logic.

Event Structure

  • Duration: 2-3 hours
  • Team size: 2-3 members
  • Platform: Online coding environment
  • Multiple rounds with increasing difficulty

Challenges Include

  • Algorithm optimization problems
  • Data structure implementations
  • Real-world engineering scenarios
  • Time-bound coding sprints
  • Debugging challenges

Programming Languages

Python, Java, C++, JavaScript, or any language of your choice (subject to platform availability)

Prizes

  • Cash prizes for winning teams
  • Certificates for all participants
  • Goodies and swag

Skills Required

  • Proficiency in at least one programming language
  • Problem-solving abilities
  • Team collaboration
  • Quick thinking and adaptability

CAD Design Competition

Overview

The CAD Design Competition challenges participants to demonstrate their computer-aided design skills by creating innovative and functional engineering designs. This event focuses on precision, creativity, and practical application of CAD software.

Competition Details

  • Duration: 3-4 hours
  • Individual or team participation (max 2 members)
  • On-site competition with provided workstations
  • Problem statement revealed on the day of event

Allowed Software

  • SolidWorks
  • AutoCAD
  • CATIA
  • Fusion 360
  • Other CAD software (subject to availability)

Evaluation Criteria

  • Design feasibility and functionality (35%)
  • Innovation and creativity (25%)
  • Technical accuracy and precision (25%)
  • Presentation and documentation (15%)

Deliverables

  • Complete 3D model
  • Technical drawings (2D)
  • Bill of materials (if applicable)
  • Brief design explanation

Prizes & Recognition

  • Cash prizes for top performers
  • Certificates from ASME
  • Recognition in event publications

Design of Centrifugal Pump

Overview

The Pump Design Challenge is a specialized mechanical engineering competition where participants design and optimize pump systems based on specific requirements. This event tests knowledge of fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, and mechanical design principles.

Competition Format

  • Duration: 4 hours
  • Team event (2-4 members)
  • Combination of theoretical and practical tasks
  • Design, calculation, and presentation phases

Challenge Components

  • Pump type selection based on application
  • Performance calculations (flow rate, head, efficiency)
  • Component design and sizing
  • Material selection
  • Cost optimization
  • System integration considerations

Required Knowledge

  • Fluid mechanics and hydraulics
  • Pump theory and types
  • Thermodynamics
  • Mechanical design fundamentals
  • Engineering calculations

Evaluation Criteria

  • Design efficiency and optimization (30%)
  • Technical calculations and accuracy (30%)
  • Innovation and problem-solving (20%)
  • Presentation quality (15%)
  • Cost-effectiveness (5%)

Tools & Resources

  • Reference materials provided
  • Standard engineering handbooks allowed
  • Calculators permitted
  • CAD software access (optional)

Prizes

  • Cash prizes for top 3 teams
  • ASME certificates
  • Trophy for the winning team
  • Opportunity for mentorship from industry experts
